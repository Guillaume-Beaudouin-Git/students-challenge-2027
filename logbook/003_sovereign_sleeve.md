## Decision

**55% of the portfolio in dollar government and supranational paper, 2.5 years on average.**

| Sleeve | Weight |
|---|---|
| Treasury bills and notes under 2 years | 25% |
| Treasuries of 2 to 5 years | 15% |
| Inflation-linked Treasuries | 5% |
| Dollar green bonds of supranational issuers, if eligible | 10% |

If the green bonds are not eligible, Treasuries of the same maturity take their place. No euro
sovereign at the open.

## Why no euro sovereign

The portfolio is valued in dollars and may not use derivatives, so it cannot hedge the euro. From
2008 to 2026, euro sovereign bonds had a volatility of 5.7% in euros and 10.3% for a dollar
investor, and they lost 0.45% on average in the worst weeks for equities, when Treasuries gained
0.56%. For this mandate a Bund is a currency position with a bond attached. France also votes on
18 April 2027.

## What is priced

- Yields: 4.12% at 3 months, 4.63% at 2 years, 4.71% at 5 years, 4.92% at 10 years.
- Federal funds futures: 4.04% for December 2026, 4.44% for June 2027, 4.42% for December 2027,
  against an effective rate of 3.88%. One rise on 9 December, a second by June, then a plateau.
- Inflation compensation over five years: 2.18% a year.

Figures: [`descriptive/market_pricing.py`](../descriptive/market_pricing.py).

## Our view

We do not fight the next rise. We expect the plateau to break sooner than priced. At a flat oil
price, Brent's change over a year goes from +69% in October 2026 to −7% in March 2027, and US
crude from +61% to −8%: energy stops adding to inflation from the March figures. Core inflation is
the open question. It rose 0.3% in October, for the third month running.

## Condition

We go to about 5 years once US core inflation is 0.2% or less two months running. Half a
point less on yields then adds about 1.3% to the allocation of our central scenario.

## Objection

*Tom: at 2.5 years we give up the protection Treasuries gave in the AI correction, when 7–10-year
bonds gained 3.2%.*

Ewan: in the oil shock the same bonds lost 2.3% while equities fell. We lengthen when the condition
is met, and at once if the "AI sell-off" scenario is declared.

## Wrong if

Brent above $110, or core inflation at 0.3% again in the January and February releases: we stay
short.
