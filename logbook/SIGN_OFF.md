# Sign-off

An entry belongs to its owner, an objection to its challenger, and a page of the application file
to the member whose name it carries. Each of us signs here, from his or her own account, after
reading the text, correcting it and agreeing to defend it. A signature is a date written on one's
own line of the tables below.

Nobody signs for someone else. If a member approves by message instead, the chair records it in the
Note column and says that he is the one recording it. An empty cell means: not signed yet.
