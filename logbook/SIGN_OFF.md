# Sign-off

An entry belongs to its owner, an objection to its challenger, and a page of the application file
to the member whose name it carries. Each of us signs below, from his or her own account, after
reading those texts, correcting them and agreeing to defend them.

To sign: write the date after "Signed on:" under your own name, and change nothing else.

Nobody signs for someone else. If a member approves by message instead, the chair writes it under
that member's name and says that he is the one recording it. A line without a date is not signed.
