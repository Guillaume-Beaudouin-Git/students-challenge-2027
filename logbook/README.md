# The Logbook

Every decision of our portfolio is written here before the order, and scored after it.

An entry gives the decision, the evidence, what the price already assumes, what would prove us
wrong and by when, and the objection of a challenger from another seat. An entry that cannot say
how it could be wrong is not filed.

approvals in [`SIGN_OFF.md`](SIGN_OFF.md).

## How an entry is scored

At each committee and on 30 April 2027, every entry gets one of four verdicts.

| | Our reason held | It did not |
|---|---|---|
| **The line gained** | **Right.** The thesis is kept. | **Lucky.** Scored as a miss. The size is not raised. |
| **The line lost** | **Early.** Kept while no exit condition is met. | **Wrong.** Did we act on the exit in time? |

Two more facts are recorded for each entry: whether it was filed before the order, and whose
objections were borne out, the human challenger's or the AI's.

## What "written before the trade" means here

The date of every version of an entry is in the history of this repository. A commit date can be
rewritten, so the fingerprints of the entries are also time-stamped by an independent authority:
see [`MANIFEST.sha256`](MANIFEST.sha256) and [`../timestamps/`](../timestamps/).

## How these entries were written

We used Claude, an AI model, to draft and revise them and to attack each draft. Entries 002 and 005
show what the attack is worth: the first draft of 002 ignored Schneider's purchase of PTC, and the
first draft of 005 showed only the periods that favoured it. Both were rewritten. Each entry is the
responsibility of its owner, who signs it.
